# Education, Agency, And Enterprise Models

## Education

Education workspaces need safe defaults, classroom-friendly limits, simple publishing, student privacy, and predictable pricing.

## Agency

Agency workspaces need multiple clients, approvals, white-label exports, custom branding, reporting, and repeatable campaign setup.

## Enterprise

Enterprise needs procurement support, audit logs, custom contracts, security review, custom domains, analytics retention, support SLAs, and future dedicated deployments.

## Shared Base

All models use the same Experience, trigger, publishing, scanner, fallback, billing, and analytics core.
