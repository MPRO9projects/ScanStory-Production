# User Personas

## Creator

Creates Experiences from printed images and videos. Needs fast upload, clear quality feedback, testing, and confidence before publishing.

## Organization Owner

Owns billing, workspaces, members, brand settings, limits, and risk. Needs plan clarity, auditability, and stable public links.

## Agency Producer

Runs many client campaigns. Needs bulk upload foundations, approval workflow, white-label options, reporting, and clean handoff.

## Educator

Creates learning material tied to posters, textbooks, flashcards, exhibits, or classroom displays. Needs simple creation, student-safe viewing, and affordable plan rules.

## Enterprise Buyer

Needs security review, procurement, support commitments, custom contracts, analytics retention, custom domains, and future dedicated deployment options.

## Viewer

Uses a phone browser to scan an image. Wants quick launch, clear permission prompts, no account requirement, and fallback if the camera or device fails.

## Admin And Support

Diagnoses failed uploads, processing errors, scanner issues, billing questions, and abuse reports without touching private camera frames.
