Consumed count is derived from live payment reservations and is never editable directly - it stays in sync automatically as reservations are created, activated, released, or expired. Reconciliation/recovery (expiring stale reservations, repairing drift) remains a CLI-only operation for now (flask expire-stale-reservations, flask reconcile-capacity-reservations).
