Ownership Review

Ownership is never inferred. A transfer moves a project only when the recipient accepts and their account has both a free project slot and enough storage; a claim is evidence, not proof.

Review order. Where a project has an explicit managing vendor, that vendor answers a claim first and admin review is the escalation - approve and reject stay unavailable until the vendor responds or the response deadline passes. Where a project has no managing vendor, direct admin review is the correct path and is available immediately. A transfer that reaches its deadline becomes EXPIRED: that closes the handover without moving ownership, and it does not cancel a linked claim.
