How ScanStory works

ScanStory connects real-world images to digital video experiences through QR and visual recognition.

  1. Choose the target photo people will point their camera at.
  2. Add the video that should play on that target.
  3. We generate a QR code for you to print or share.
  4. Your viewer scans the QR code on their phone.
  5. Your viewer points the camera at the target.
  6. The video plays over the target. That is the ScanStory.
Please give your ScanStory a name.

2 Add your photo & video

For each content set: pick the photo people will scan, the video that plays when they scan it, and (optionally) adjust the exact area that triggers it.

  • Photo: JPG or PNG. Detailed and high-contrast works best — a poster, label, artwork or printed card. Avoid blank, blurry or heavily reflective surfaces.
  • Video: MP4 or MOV. Keep it short and in the same shape as your photo so it sits neatly on top.
  • After you create: background processing prepares your photo for recognition. Your QR code is ready immediately and never changes.
