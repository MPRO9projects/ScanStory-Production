Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ScanStory's WebAR platform.

ScanStory is a WebAR experience platform that lets you create augmented reality content without any app or technical skills. You upload a photo and a video, generate a QR scan code, and anyone who scans the QR and points their camera at the photo will see the video overlay appear directly on top of the image — all inside a mobile browser.
WebAR is browser-based augmented reality that works without installing a mobile app. Users open an AR experience through a link or QR code, allow camera access, and view digital content such as videos or 3D overlays directly through their mobile browser.
ScanStory uses image recognition and WebAR technology. After you upload a photo and video, our system processes the image and extracts visual features. When a user scans your QR code and points their camera at the matching photo, the system recognises the image in real time and overlays the video on top of it using perspective transformation — creating a seamless augmented reality experience.
An AR QR code is a scannable code that opens an augmented reality experience. In ScanStory, the QR code opens a browser-based AR scanner. When the user points the camera at the target image, the linked video appears as an AR overlay.
No app download is needed. ScanStory works entirely inside a standard mobile browser. The viewer scans your QR code, the scanner page opens in their browser, and the AR experience runs directly on their phone — no installation, no friction.
Absolutely. AR business cards are one of ScanStory's most popular use cases. Upload your business card design as the target image, attach an introduction or portfolio video, and print the QR code on the back. When someone scans it and points their camera at the card, your video plays directly over the card — making a powerful first impression.
Yes. ScanStory is ideal for educators and edtech creators. Attach video lessons or animations to textbook pages, flashcards, or learning posters. When students scan the QR code and point their camera at the page, the AR lesson plays — making learning more interactive and engaging without any specialist hardware.
After uploading, ScanStory processes your image to extract visual features used for AR detection, and prepares your video for streaming. A unique QR scan code is generated for your project. You can download, share, or print this QR code. Anyone who scans it will be taken to your AR scanner page where the video overlays on the image in real time.
