Claim a ScanStory you scanned

Scanned a QR code for a ScanStory that should be yours - printed for you, made by a vendor on your behalf, or handed over outside the app? Paste its link here and we will check whether you can file an ownership review request for it.

Open the QR code with any phone camera and copy the link it goes to.

Filing a request never transfers a ScanStory and never changes anything on its own. It goes to the current owner - or to the vendor who manages the ScanStory for them - to answer first, and to the ScanStory team afterwards if there is no vendor or no answer. Ownership moves only when a handover is opened and the receiving account accepts it.
