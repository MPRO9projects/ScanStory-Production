Introduction

ScanStory is a WebAR platform by Namma Mysore Foundation. You upload a photo and video, get a QR code, and anyone who scans that QR and points their camera at the photo sees the video overlay in AR — no app needed.

QR Code Usage

Each project generates a unique QR code that opens your AR scanner page.

  • QR codes are generated for your project access only — do not share them for unintended purposes.

  • QR code access stops if your plan expires or your account is suspended.

Always download a fresh QR code after updating your project — old printed codes may point to outdated content.

AR Scanner & Camera Permission

The ScanStory scanner runs in your mobile browser using your device camera.

  • Camera permission is required — the scanner will not work without it.
  • The page must be opened over HTTPS. Camera access is blocked on plain HTTP.
  • Works best on Chrome (Android) and Safari (iOS). Older or unsupported browsers may have issues.
  • Detection quality depends on lighting, camera resolution, internet speed, and the quality of your uploaded image.
⚠️ ScanStory is not responsible for detection failures caused by poor lighting, low-resolution cameras, or slow internet connections.
